Privacy Policy

MS Clipper · last updated 21 October 2026

MS Clipper is a private tool used only by its owner on the owner's own computer.

What data the app accesses

  • With your permission (Google sign-in), the app uploads videos to your YouTube channel and reads basic statistics of those videos (views, likes, comments count).
  • It does not read your email, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How data is stored

Access tokens and video statistics are stored only locally on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored tokens can be deleted on request.
